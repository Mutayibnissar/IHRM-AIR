Official standard · Unit 4

International Labour Organization — MNE Declaration

The ILO’s tripartite declaration provides guidance for multinational and national enterprises, governments, and employer and worker organisations. Topics include employment, training, working conditions, freedom of association, collective bargaining and industrial relations.

  • Institutional source: ILO
  • Latest amendment identified by the ILO: 2022
  • Access: official public guidance and publication

How to use sources responsibly

  1. Confirm the exact topic and terminology in your University of Kashmir syllabus and prescribed text.
  2. Prefer the original textbook, journal article, official labour standard or government publication over unsourced summaries.
  3. For academic papers, record author, year, title, journal, volume/pages and DOI or stable URL.
  4. Separate a source’s finding from your own example or interpretation; do not treat a source as proof of claims it does not discuss.
  5. Questions in this platform stay marked draft until their answer and explanation are checked against appropriate sources.
